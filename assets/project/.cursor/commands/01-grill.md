Interview me about this idea before writing code: [IDEA].

Ask one question at a time. Prioritize:
- Target users and the problem being solved.
- Primary user journey and first meaningful action.
- Inputs, outputs and data to store.
- Roles, permissions and ownership of data.
- Failure states, edge cases and recovery.
- Integrations, constraints and non-goals.
- Success criteria and how we will validate them.

Do not ask questions already answered. When sufficiently clear, summarize the product in 10 concise points, list unresolved assumptions, and ask me to confirm the summary. Do not create or edit files.
