# Incident Analysis

## Procedure
1. Establish incident window, affected systems and user impact.
2. Build a timeline from logs, alerts, deploys and reports.
3. Separate facts, hypotheses and unknowns.
4. Identify detection, escalation, containment and recovery actions.
5. Trace technical and organizational contributing factors without blame.
6. Validate recovery and check for residual impact or data inconsistency.
7. Recommend immediate, short-term and systemic prevention actions.
8. Assign measurable follow-up items and observability improvements.

## Guardrails
Do not speculate as fact, expose sensitive data or make production changes without approval.

## Deliverable
Impact, timeline, root cause/confidence, contributing factors, recovery validation, follow-ups and open questions.
